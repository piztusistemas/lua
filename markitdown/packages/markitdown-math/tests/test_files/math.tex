\section*{Quadratic formula}
The roots of $ax^2+bx+c=0$ are given by
\[ x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}. \]
Moreover, $\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}$ and
\[ \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}. \]
